How I Think

Pragmatic principles for building real products.

Technology should solve a product or operational problem, not exist for its own sake.

Architecture should reduce future constraints without displaying unnecessary complexity.

Small teams need ownership and clarity more than ceremony.

Technical leadership is stronger when it understands implementation details.

Reliability, security, and scalability are product qualities.

Good processes remove uncertainty without slowing the team down.
